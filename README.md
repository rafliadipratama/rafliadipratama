### Featured Systems

#### 🎬 [LiveEuy](https://github.com/rafliadipratama/LiveEuy) — Adaptive HLS Video Streaming
High-throughput video streaming platform with ambient canvas illumination.
- **Frontend:** React 18 + HLS.js with real-time canvas ambient glow rendered smoothly at 60 FPS.
- **Backend Core:** Go (Golang) microservice for concurrent video chunk delivery with zero-copy I/O streaming.
- **Storage & Cache:** Nginx reverse proxy edge caching backed by Redis and PostgreSQL.

#### 🏢 Pharmaceutical e-Document System — Quality & Compliance Suite
Enterprise document governance platform complying with pharmaceutical CPOB regulations.
- Enforces multi-tier approval lifecycles for SOPs, CAPA, Deviations, and Change Control.
- Implements 6-tier role-based access control (Spatie RBAC) and cryptographic electronic signature verification.
- *Tech:* `Laravel 12` · `PHP 8.2` · `MySQL` · `Spatie RBAC` · `Tailwind CSS`

#### 🛒 [Marketplace Solas](https://gitlab.com/rafliadipratama/marketplace-solas) — Omnichannel & Concurrency Engine
Internal enterprise pharmaceutical marketplace with external e-commerce integrations.
- Bi-directional inventory synchronization with Shopee & Tokopedia Open APIs.
- Redis distributed mutex locking (`SETNX` / Redlock) to eliminate race conditions and overselling during flash sales.
- *Tech:* `Laravel 12` · `Redis Mutex` · `Shopee/Tokopedia API` · `Alpine.js` · `MySQL`

#### 🌐 [Solas Corporate & HR Portal](https://solas.id) — Corporate Platform & Talent Assessment
Official corporate web portal integrated with an automated recruitment and psychometric testing engine.
- Automated candidate evaluation pipeline featuring online DISC and aptitude assessments.
- Real-time application tracking updates delivered via WhatsApp Business API.
- *Tech:* `Laravel 12` · `WhatsApp API` · `Alpine.js` · `Tailwind CSS` · `GitLab CI/CD`

#### 🎵 [MusicEuy](https://github.com/rafliadipratama/MusicEuy) — Web Audio Streaming & Visualizer
Audio player with real-time frequency spectrum analysis and local/cloud playlist management.
- Web Audio API audio graph analyzer rendering synchronized 60 FPS frequency bars on HTML5 Canvas.
- *Tech:* `React` · `Web Audio API` · `HTML5 Canvas` · `Tailwind CSS`
